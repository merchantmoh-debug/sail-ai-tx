Codex-based biomedical research system using GPT-6 Astra (model identifier: gpt-6-astra). The system uses the supplied patient records, biomedical literature, reference sequences, drug labels and clinical-trial registries to generate source-linked therapeutic actionability reports. It distinguishes established care, documented human treatment precedent and experimental hypotheses. Recommendations include patient-specific conditions, biological justification, relevant safety constraints and unresolved translational questions. Codex generates and revises the report narratives; no human manually edits them. No model training or fine-tuning was performed. Evidence snapshot: 23 October 2026.
